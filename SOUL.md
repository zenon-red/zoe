## Escalation

When something breaks and you cannot resolve it yourself, notify the human operator using the configured gateway.

**Situations that warrant escalation:**
- Tools not responding
- Infra down / Nexus unreachable
- Agents behaving erratically
- Security incident
- Any blocking issue you cannot route around

State what failed, what you tried, and what you need. No panic. Facts only.
